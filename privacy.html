Website Information

Privacy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

The BU Commodities Trading Club ("BUCTC," "we," "us," or "the Club") is a student organization at Boston University. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the BUCTC website.

This website is maintained by the Club and is separate from Boston University's official websites and systems.

Information You Provide

Mailing List

The website provides a mailing-list signup form. When you use this form, we collect the information you choose to submit, which currently includes your name and email address. We use this information to manage the Club's mailing list and to send communications concerning meetings, events, competitions, market discussions, Club announcements, and other Club-related activities and opportunities.

Mailing-list subscriptions are managed using MailerLite, a third-party email marketing service. Information submitted through the mailing-list form is therefore processed using MailerLite's services and is also subject to MailerLite's applicable privacy practices. You may unsubscribe from Club mailing-list communications at any time by using the unsubscribe option provided in those emails. Joining the mailing list does not make you an official member of BUCTC.

Official Club Membership

Official Club membership is managed separately through Boston University's designated student-organization platform, TerrierCentral. When you follow a membership link from this website, you leave the BUCTC website and interact with a Boston University service. Information you submit through TerrierCentral is handled according to Boston University's applicable policies and practices rather than this Privacy Policy.

Website Analytics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to help us understand general website usage and improve the site. According to Cloudflare, Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use client-side state such as cookies or local storage to collect usage metrics and does not fingerprint individual visitors for the purpose of providing analytics. Cloudflare operates independently of BUCTC, and its services and privacy practices may change over time.

External Websites and Services

This website contains links to websites and services operated by third parties, including Boston University services, MailerLite, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, news sources, market-related resources, and other external websites. When you follow an external link, your interaction with that website or service is governed by the third party's own terms, privacy policies, and practices. BUCTC does not control the operation, security, availability, accessibility, or privacy practices of third-party websites and services. The presence of a link on this website does not necessarily indicate endorsement of the third party, its content, or its services by BUCTC or Boston University.

Information We Do Not Collect

Except for information voluntarily submitted through features such as the mailing-list form, BUCTC does not intend to use this website to collect sensitive personal information, financial account information, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or passwords. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the website or mailing-list form.

Data Requests and Questions

If you have a question about information you submitted directly through the BUCTC website or would like to request that the Club stop using mailing-list information associated with you, please contact BUCTC using the contact information provided on this website. Requests involving information submitted directly to Boston University, TerrierCentral, MailerLite, or another third-party service may also be subject to that organization's own procedures and policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website, Club activities, or the services we use change. When material changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
