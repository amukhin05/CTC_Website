Website Information

Privacy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This privacy notice explains how this website handles information. BU Commodities Trading Club is a student organization at Boston University.

Information collected on this website

The website itself does not currently provide user accounts or a club-operated contact form. The site does use a third-party form website for club membership or interest submissions. Information entered into that form will be provided by you directly to third-party provider and made available to club officers who have access to the form responses. BUCTC does not, and cannot, control the privacy policy of the third-party website currently used.

Membership forms

The specific information requested will be shown on the third-party form before you submit it. Do not submit information you do not want to provide. The third-party's website's own terms and privacy practices apply to its service in addition to the club's handling of responses. BUCTC does not, and cannot, control the privacy policy of the third-party website currently used.

Analytics

This site utilizes Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website usage. Cloudflare, as of the date listed at the top of this statement, states that Web Analytics does not use cookies, local storage, or fingerprinting to collect its usage metrics. Analytics activation is managed through the site's Cloudflare Pages project rather than through a club-built tracking system. Cloudflares policies are subject to change, and as such, this statement is only accurate as of the publishing date listed at the top of the statement.

External links

The site links to third-party services and websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those third parties, and not by this website. BUCTC currently does not have any affiliation with any party owning any of these links, with the exception of any Boston University affiliated links.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the site begins collecting different information, when the membership form is finalized, or when the club's tools change. These changes will be relfected on this page, and are not subject to notice.
