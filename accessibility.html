Website Information

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 12, 2026

BU Commodities Trading Club wants this website to be usable by as many people as reasonably possible. This statement describes the site as it currently exists and does not claim formal conformance with WCAG or any other accessibility standard.

Current approach

The site uses native HTML links and navigation, heading structure, text labels for primary controls, and alternative text for meaningful images. Decorative images may, but do not necessarily, intentionally use empty alternative text so they are skipped by assistive technology.

Known limitations

The site is consistently under development and is used exclusively for the purposes realted to a university club, and therefore has not undergone a formal third-party accessibility audit. Some links may be placeholders while club destinations such as membership forms and communication channels, as well as news links or any other links on this wesbite, are undergoing maintenance. Third-party services linked from this site may have accessibility behavior that BUCTC does not, and cannot, control.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please notify the one of the contact channels listed at the bottom of the webiste or anywhere else on the website. If a dedicated contact location cannot be found on this website, then it should be interpreted that the website is currently undergoing maintenance and is not intended, and that some form of contact information will be published once the maintenance finalizes.

Ongoing review

This statement will be updated in accordance to the relevant changes on this website.
