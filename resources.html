Resources

A beginner-friendly place to start.

You do not need prior experience. These resources will help you learn the basic vocabulary, research commodity markets, and follow the forces that influence prices.

Knowledge Databse

What Should You Learn Next?

Basic Vocabulary

How to Describe and Analyze Commodify Markets?

Books and Podcasts

Go deeper.

Books

Commodities 101 Morgan Downey - four different free online books on Oil, NatGas, Power, and Commodities in general. Commodity Markets: Evolution, Challenges, and Policies John Baffes & Peter Nagle — commodity demand, price cycles, energy, metals, agriculture, shocks, and the global economy.

Podcasts

Odd Lots Bloomberg conversations about markets, economics, supply chains, energy, and financial systems The HC Commodities Podcast Interviews with professionals across energy, commodities, trading, and physical markets

Useful Websites

Research the market.

CME Group Commodities

Energy Data

U.S. Energy Information Administration

Metals Data

London Metal Exchange

Follow copper, aluminium, nickel, zinc, lead, tin, and other industrial-metal markets.

Review monthly global supply-and-demand forecasts for major agricultural commodities.

Market Positioning

Commitments of Traders

Examine weekly futures positioning by producers, merchants, funds, and other trader groups.

Global Data

Access commodity prices, forecasts, outlook reports, and the monthly Commodity Price Data Pink Sheet.
